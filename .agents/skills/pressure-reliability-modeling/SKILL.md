# Pressure Reliability Modeling

## Workflow
Compute pressure using only pre-ball state; separately identify match difficulty and leverage. Establish role/phase/league normal baselines and compare high-pressure outcomes against player's usual performance. Use fair context-adjusted run/wicket expectations, reliability across matches, empirical-Bayes shrinkage and uncertainty. Rank eligible batters and bowlers independently. Avoid post-ball leakage and reputation selection. Model training uses chronological match-grouped splits; compare to simple baseline. Provide explanations, rank sensitivity, confidence and source match references. Do not turn modeled scores into psychiatric/psychological claims.
## Exit gate
Exactly ten top batters and ten top bowlers, each from approved master list and eligible dropdown list when known; methodology and reported metrics generated by code, not typed placeholders.
