# Media Discovery & Rights

1. Player portrait SOURCE HARD RULE: locate each player via an official `iplt20.com` player/profile page. Only the official IPL site's image asset referenced there (including its legitimate CDN) can be associated with that player ID. This is a source/provenance rule, not a blanket copyright licence.
2. Do not substitute RR homepage images, Google image search thumbnails, ESPNcricinfo, news media, Wikipedia, unofficial social media, or AI imagery for player portraits. If IPL has no reliable portrait, render an honest no-photo silhouette/name treatment.
3. User accepts video of ANY cricket team and event; prefer Rajasthan Royals batting/bowling/celebrations for a RR-themed hero. Unrelated footage must be context-neutral and not falsely tagged as RR.
4. For each picture/video record official source page URL, actual media/CDN URL, actor or match identity, season/event, licence/permission, if rehosting is allowed, whether derivative editing/background removal is allowed, attribution and date checked.
5. BCCI / IPL terms do not grant automatic reuse. No ripping streams or bypassing access restrictions. Use permitted embeds where possible and eligible original/licensed footage otherwise.
6. Build `assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.json` and `docs/assets/MEDIA_RIGHTS_AND_GAPS.md`; verify provenance with browser evidence. Keep unlicensed review-only captures out of production bundles.
7. For the 2008 trophy editorial section, non-player archival imagery may come from another legitimately licensed source. Such historical figures are not added to the selectable player list.
## Exit gate
Every player portrait has authoritative IPL page provenance or a declared missing portrait; every public media asset has permitted use and adaptation status, or is withheld.
