## Evidence and limits

The corrected frame visually shows front-facing desks and pupils, the single retained hero at the nearest desk, and an ordinary rear wall instead of the teaching board behind their backs. That removes the concrete contradiction in V2.

**This frame does not itself show the offscreen front board/teacher**, so their exact location is a blocking proposal established by the layout, not proof from a second rendered angle. The plan is schematic, not measured room dimensions. There are thirteen background students plus the hero in the working composition; several are partly occluded.

Lower seated anatomy is hidden by the desk. This is a spatial/blocking check, not a completed seated-rig or motion-contact audit. Background students remain still artwork. The exact eyeline turn toward the newly established teacher position still needs to be revised and checked in motion if the user later approves another opening render.
