## 1. Colour Rhythm & Environmental Alternation

The current single-tone near-black theme is replaced by a deliberate **cinematic light/dark rhythmic cadence**:

```
[Chapter 1: Hero]          Dark, atmospheric navy (#080C1F / #13245B) + full-bleed cricket video montage
        ↓
[Chapter 2: Squad Band]    Vivid rhythmic panels (#E81274 / #203A8C / #29134F / #FFE7F0) with large human cutouts
        ↓
[Chapter 3: Manifesto]     Light & airy off-white canvas (#F8F5F4) with oversized black typography
        ↓
[Chapter 4: 2008 Museum]   Layered monochrome archival photographs + deep Rajasthan navy (#080C1F)
        ↓
[Chapter 5: Orbit Quiz]    Expansive, glowing cream/pale-pink canvas (#FAF6F0 / #FFE7F0) with orbiting cutouts
        ↓
[Chapter 6: Final CTA]     Rich royal pink/navy gradient (#E81274 to #13245B) transitioning into an elegant footer
```

### Brand Colour Palette
- **Royal Pink:** `#E81274` (Hero accents, active indicators, primary button fill)
- **Vibrant Magenta:** `#EF278C` (Gradient highlights, secondary accents)
- **Royal Navy:** `#13245B` (Deep atmospheric surfaces, container borders)
- **Deep Purple:** `#29134F` (Shadow layers, card gradients)
- **Off-White Canvas:** `#F8F5F4` (Editorial manifesto background)
- **Warm Cream / Pale Pink:** `#FAF6F0` / `#FFE7F0` (Orbiting quiz canvas)
- **Subtle Pale Gold:** `#F5B400` / `#F2E67C` (Trophy moments, AUGE diamond cursor follower)
- **Midnight Ink:** `#080C1F` (Base dark contrast canvas)

## 2. Typography Hierarchy

- **Cinematic Display:** `Space Grotesk` / `Inter`, bold/black weight, uppercase, tracking tight (`-0.03em`).
  - Hero Headline: `80px – 110px` on desktop.
  - Section Headlines: `48px – 72px`.
- **Editorial Sub-Headings:** `Inter`, medium/semibold weight, `20px – 28px`.
- **Body & Storytelling:** `Inter`, light/regular weight, `16px – 18px`, relaxed line-height (`1.65`).
- **Telemetry & Metrics:** `JetBrains Mono`, regular/bold, uppercase, tracking wide (`+0.05em`). *Restricted exclusively to actual numbers, leverage indices, and IDs.*
